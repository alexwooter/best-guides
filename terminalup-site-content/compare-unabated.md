# TerminalUp vs Unabated

**Unabated is an odds screen and pricing toolkit built for sharp bettors, known for its market-based fair line. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. Unabated helps you decide what a price should be. TerminalUp is where the order goes once you have decided.**

| | Unabated | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Odds screen and pricing tools for sharp bettors | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, at each sportsbook | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per sportsbook | One |
| Pricing | Paid subscription plans | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Bettors who price markets themselves | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose Unabated if

You build your own numbers, you want a clean screen and a fair line to measure against, and you place your own bets.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You already know the price you want and you need it routed across venues from one account, with size.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp an Unabated alternative?**
They do different jobs. Unabated is a screen and pricing toolkit. TerminalUp compares prices and routes the order.

**Does TerminalUp publish a fair line?**
TerminalUp shows the prices available across connected venues and routes to the best one. It is not a handicapping or picks service.

**What does TerminalUp cost compared with a screen subscription?**
TerminalUp charges 0.5% commission on trades and has no subscription.
