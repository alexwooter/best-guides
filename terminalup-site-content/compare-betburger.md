# TerminalUp vs BetBurger

**BetBurger is a subscription scanner that surfaces arbitrage and value opportunities across a long list of bookmakers. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. BetBurger alerts you and you place every leg yourself. TerminalUp shows the prices and routes the order.**

| | BetBurger | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Arbitrage and value scanner | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, leg by leg, at each bookmaker | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per bookmaker | One |
| Pricing | Monthly subscription | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Arbitrage bettors who want wide bookmaker coverage | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose BetBurger if

You want fast arbitrage alerts across a very wide set of bookmakers and you place each leg yourself.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want cross-venue prices on one board, one account and orders routed for you.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp a BetBurger alternative?**
For traders who want execution across venues from one account, yes. For a pure arbitrage scanner, BetBurger is the closer fit.

**Does TerminalUp send arbitrage alerts?**
No. TerminalUp is a trading terminal. It shows the prices across connected venues and routes orders.

**What does TerminalUp cost?**
0.5% commission on trades, no subscription, with a $10,000 minimum deposit.
