# TerminalUp vs Underdog

**Underdog is a major US sports app with event contracts on a CFTC-regulated exchange. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** Underdog is listed in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

Underdog is a US sports app offering predictions, pick'em and season-long fantasy, with event contracts through Underdog Exchange.

## FAQ

**Is Underdog on TerminalUp?**

**Is TerminalUp a prediction market?**
No. TerminalUp is trading software. It routes orders to sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, holds no book and never takes the other side of a trade.

0.5% commission on trades, no subscription, with a $10,000 minimum deposit.
