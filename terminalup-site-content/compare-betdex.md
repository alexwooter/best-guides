# TerminalUp vs BetDEX

**BetDEX is a betting exchange known for low commission. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** BetDEX is listed as a confirmed venue in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What BetDEX does well

BetDEX is a betting exchange, with a sportsbook alongside it.

- Exchange pricing set by its users.
- Low commission with no premium charge, per BetDEX.
- An exchange and a sportsbook in one account.

## Not either/or

TerminalUp does not compete with BetDEX. It is software that sits on top of venues and compares their prices. The current directory is at https://terminalup.com/partners.
