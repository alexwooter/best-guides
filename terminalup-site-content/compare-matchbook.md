# TerminalUp vs Matchbook

**Matchbook is one exchange. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** Matchbook is a betting exchange with an order book on major sports. Matchbook is listed as a confirmed venue in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## Choose Matchbook directly if

You are outside the US and want to trade on Matchbook's exchange alone.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want prices compared across venues on every order, from one account.

## FAQ

**Is Matchbook on TerminalUp?**
Matchbook is listed as a confirmed venue in the TerminalUp directory. The current list is at https://terminalup.com/partners.

**Is TerminalUp an exchange?**
No. TerminalUp is trading software. It routes orders to sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, holds no book and never takes the other side of a trade.

**What does TerminalUp cost?**
0.5% commission on trades, no subscription, with a $10,000 minimum deposit.
