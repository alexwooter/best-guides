# TerminalUp vs Betstamp

**Betstamp is an odds screen and bet tracker with a free tier. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. Betstamp is a good way to see the best price on a slate and log what you bet. TerminalUp is built for traders who want the order placed across venues for them.**

| | Betstamp | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Odds screen and bet tracker | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, at each sportsbook | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per sportsbook | One |
| Pricing | Free tier, paid upgrades | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Bettors who want a free screen and a tracker in one place | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose Betstamp if

You want a free way to shop lines and track your bets, and you place each bet yourself.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want comparison, routing and one portfolio across venues from a single account.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp a Betstamp alternative?**
Only if you need execution. Betstamp shows prices and tracks bets. TerminalUp compares prices and routes the order.

**Is TerminalUp free like Betstamp's free tier?**
No. TerminalUp charges 0.5% commission on trades and requires a $10,000 minimum deposit.

**Does TerminalUp track positions?**
Yes. Positions and profit and loss from every venue sit in one portfolio.
