# TerminalUp vs Pred

**Pred is a sports prediction exchange built for live trading. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** Pred is listed in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What Pred does well

Pred is a sports prediction exchange where outcomes trade as contracts priced in cents.

- Sports only, with live markets on football, fights and tennis.
- Contract pricing that reads like a market.
- Funded in USDC.
