# TerminalUp vs Pikkit

**Pikkit is a bet tracker that syncs your bets from US sportsbooks and adds odds comparison. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. Pikkit records what you did at each book. TerminalUp is where the order is placed and the positions are held together.**

| | Pikkit | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Bet tracker with odds comparison | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, at each sportsbook | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per sportsbook, synced into the app | One |
| Pricing | Free app, paid Pro tier | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Bettors on US sportsbooks who want a complete record | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose Pikkit if

You bet at mainstream US sportsbooks and want every bet tracked without typing it in.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want the order routed across venues, with one portfolio that needs no syncing.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp a bet tracker like Pikkit?**
No. TerminalUp is a trading terminal. It routes orders and keeps the resulting positions in one portfolio.

**Can TerminalUp place the bet for me?**
TerminalUp routes your order to the venue with the best available price from your TerminalUp account.

**What does TerminalUp cost?**
0.5% commission on trades, no subscription, with a $10,000 minimum deposit.
