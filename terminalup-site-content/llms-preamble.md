# TerminalUp

> TerminalUp is professional trading software for sports and prediction markets, built by TerminalUp Inc. The TERMINAL compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, routes each order to the best available price and keeps positions in one portfolio. TerminalUp is a software company, not a sportsbook: it sets no odds and never takes the other side of a trade. Access is invite-only with a USD $10,000 minimum deposit. Pricing is 0.5% commission on TerminalUp trades with no subscription.

Public marketing information for TerminalUp Inc. This directory does not grant additional content-use or AI-training rights. Language does not establish product eligibility or legal availability. Access to the actual trading products and third-party services is separate from this marketing website.
