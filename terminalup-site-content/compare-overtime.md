# TerminalUp vs Overtime

**Overtime is a fully on-chain sportsbook. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** Overtime is listed in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What Overtime does well

Overtime is an on-chain sportsbook with live markets, player props, futures and parlays.

- Fully on-chain and non-custodial.
- Live markets, player props, futures and parlays.
- Transparent settlement on a public blockchain.

## Not either/or

TerminalUp does not compete with Overtime. It is software that sits on top of venues and compares their prices. The current directory is at https://terminalup.com/partners.

## FAQ

**Is Overtime on TerminalUp?**
Overtime is listed in the TerminalUp directory. The current list is at https://terminalup.com/partners.

**Is TerminalUp a sportsbook?**
No. TerminalUp is trading software. It routes orders to sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, holds no book and never takes the other side of a trade.

**What does TerminalUp cost?**
0.5% commission on trades, no subscription, with a $10,000 minimum deposit.
