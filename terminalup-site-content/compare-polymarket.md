# TerminalUp vs Polymarket

**Polymarket is one of the deepest prediction markets in the world. TerminalUp is trading software that puts Polymarket on one board with other sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price.** Polymarket is live on TerminalUp. When Polymarket has the best price, the order routes there. When another venue has it, the order goes there instead. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What Polymarket does well

Polymarket is a prediction market with deep books on major events and growing sports coverage.

- Some of the deepest prediction market liquidity anywhere on major events.
- Markets open fast on almost anything people want to trade.
- Transparent on-chain settlement.

## Not either/or

TerminalUp does not compete with Polymarket for your deposit. It sends orders to Polymarket when Polymarket has the price. The only question is whether you check Polymarket against other venues by hand or let routing do it.

| | Polymarket | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Prediction market | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Prices you see | Polymarket's order book | Prices across the venues in its directory |
| Where the order goes | Polymarket | The venue with the best available price |
| Accounts you need | A Polymarket account | One TerminalUp account |
| Fees | Set by Polymarket | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |

## Choose Polymarket directly if

You want Polymarket's markets and liquidity and you are happy trading there alone.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want Polymarket compared with other venues on every order, from one account.

## FAQ

**Can I trade Polymarket through TerminalUp?**
Yes. Polymarket is a live venue on TerminalUp.

**Do I need my own Polymarket account?**
No. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

**Is TerminalUp a prediction market?**
No. TerminalUp is trading software. It routes orders to sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, holds no book and never takes the other side of a trade.
