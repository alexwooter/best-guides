# TerminalUp vs OddsJam

**OddsJam is a subscription odds screen that surfaces positive expected value and arbitrage prices across sportsbooks. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. With OddsJam you see where the price is and place the bet yourself at that book. With TerminalUp you see the comparison and the order is routed for you.**

| | OddsJam | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Odds comparison, +EV and arbitrage screen | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, at each sportsbook | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per sportsbook | One |
| Pricing | Monthly subscription, tiered by plan | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Bettors who shop lines and place their own bets | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose OddsJam if

You trade under $10,000, you want +EV and arbitrage alerts, and you are fine placing each bet yourself.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size, you want one account instead of one per book, and you want the order routed for you.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp an OddsJam alternative?**
For traders who want execution and not only a screen, yes. OddsJam finds prices. TerminalUp compares prices and routes the order.

**Can I use both?**
Yes. A screen for finding prices and a terminal for routing orders do different jobs.

**Does TerminalUp have a subscription like OddsJam?**
No. TerminalUp charges 0.5% commission on trades and has no subscription.
