# TerminalUp fees

**TerminalUp charges 0.5% commission on TerminalUp trades.** On a winning trade the commission is 0.5% of the profit. On a losing trade it is 0.5% of the amount risked. There is no subscription. The $10,000 minimum deposit is your trading balance, not a charge.

| | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- |
| Commission on a win | 0.5% of the profit |
| Commission on a loss | 0.5% of the amount risked |
| Monthly subscription | None |
| Minimum deposit | $10,000. It is your balance, not a fee. |
| Bonuses or promotions | None. TerminalUp is not a sportsbook. |

## Two examples

- You risk $10,000 and the trade wins $9,000. Commission is 0.5% of $9,000, which is $45.
- You risk $10,000 and the trade loses. Commission is 0.5% of $10,000, which is $50.

## Why commission and not a subscription

A subscription odds screen costs the same whether you trade or not. TerminalUp earns only when you trade. It earns the same rate whether you win or lose, and it never takes the other side of your trade.
