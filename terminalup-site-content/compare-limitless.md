# TerminalUp vs Limitless

**Limitless is a fast prediction market across crypto, sports and world events. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** Limitless is listed in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What Limitless does well

Limitless is a prediction market for crypto, sports, esports, finance and politics.

- Wide coverage: crypto, sports, esports, finance and politics.
- Short-dated markets for traders who want quick outcomes.
- A social trading experience with a leaderboard and a feed.
