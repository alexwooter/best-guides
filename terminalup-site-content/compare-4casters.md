# TerminalUp vs 4casters

**4casters is a peer-to-peer sports betting exchange with a clean, market-style board. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price from one account.** 4casters is listed as a confirmed venue in the TerminalUp directory. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What 4casters does well

4casters is a peer-to-peer sports betting exchange that shows the matched volume on every market.

- Peer-to-peer matching, with matched volume shown on every market.
- A wide board: NFL, college football, NBA, NHL, soccer, tennis, golf, fights and esports.
- Spread, moneyline and total shown side by side.

## Not either/or

TerminalUp does not compete with 4casters. It is software that sits on top of venues and compares their prices. The current directory is at https://terminalup.com/partners.
