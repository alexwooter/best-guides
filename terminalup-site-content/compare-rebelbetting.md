# TerminalUp vs RebelBetting

**RebelBetting is subscription software that scans bookmakers for arbitrage and value bets and shows the stakes for each leg. TerminalUp is trading software that compares prices across sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes the order from one account. RebelBetting finds the opportunity and you place each leg yourself. TerminalUp shows the prices and routes the order.**

| | RebelBetting | TerminalUp |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Arbitrage and value betting scanner | Cross-venue trading terminal with order routing |
| Placing the trade | You, leg by leg, at each bookmaker | Routed from one TerminalUp account |
| Accounts you need | One per bookmaker | One |
| Pricing | Monthly subscription | 0.5% commission on trades, no subscription |
| Access | Open sign-up | Invite-only, $10,000 minimum deposit |
| Best for | Bettors who want a dedicated arbitrage and value scanner | Traders, teams and syndicates trading with size |

## Choose RebelBetting if

You want arbitrage and value alerts across many bookmakers and you are set up to place each leg yourself.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want one account, cross-venue prices on one board and orders routed for you.

## FAQ

**Is TerminalUp arbitrage software?**
No. TerminalUp is a trading terminal. It shows the prices across connected venues and routes orders. It does not sell arbitrage alerts.

**Is TerminalUp a RebelBetting alternative?**
For traders who want execution across venues from one account, yes. For a pure arbitrage scanner, RebelBetting is the closer fit.

**Does TerminalUp charge a subscription?**
No. TerminalUp charges 0.5% commission on trades.
