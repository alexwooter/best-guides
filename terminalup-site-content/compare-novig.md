# TerminalUp vs Novig

**Novig is one of the best peer-to-peer sports exchanges in the US. TerminalUp is trading software that puts Novig on one board with other sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets and routes each order to the best available price.** Novig is live on TerminalUp. When Novig has the best price, the order routes there. When another venue has it, the order goes there instead. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

## What Novig does well

Novig is a US sports exchange where users trade against each other instead of a house.

- Peer-to-peer pricing with no house on the other side.
- A clean, fast app built for US bettors who think in prices.
- An order book that shows the price and the size.

## Not either/or

TerminalUp does not compete with Novig for your deposit. It sends orders to Novig when Novig has the price. The only question is whether you check Novig against other venues by hand or let routing do it.

## Choose Novig directly if

You only want Novig's markets and you trade below TerminalUp's $10,000 minimum.

## Choose TerminalUp if

You trade with size and want Novig compared with other venues on every order, from one account.

## FAQ

**Can I trade Novig through TerminalUp?**
Yes. Novig is a live venue on TerminalUp.

**Do I need my own Novig account?**
No. You fund one TerminalUp account. Orders route to venues from it.

**Is TerminalUp an exchange?**
No. TerminalUp is trading software. It routes orders to sportsbooks, betting exchanges and prediction markets, holds no book and never takes the other side of a trade.
